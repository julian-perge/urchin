# Spec: hub

Source issue: [#107](https://github.com/julian-perge/urchin/issues/107). Module `hub` of [the capability map](107-capability-map.md). It depends on [ui-foundation](107-ui-foundation.md): the viewport, `FloatBox`, the rect test, the compare tool, the chrome art from #161 and the strings extractor (Part 7).

Commands, project structure and code style are in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md), `docs/agents/godot.md` and `docs/agents/art-extraction.md`. Terms are in [GLOSSARY.md](../GLOSSARY.md).

This spec covers the Hub half of [#16](https://github.com/julian-perge/urchin/issues/16) (info spots and the post-battle hint cards) and the hub's part of the piece inventory. The World map is a separate Screen and is not in this module.

## Objective

Rebuild the Hub, the original's root frame `Navigation` (181), as Godot scenes on containers at the SWF's rects. The Hub is: the zone art with its orbs and info spots, the bottom bar with the hotbar and the zone progress, and the info card.

Success is that the Hub, its hotbar and its card sit where the SWF places them, the hotbar and orb tooltips read the original's strings, and the info spots and hint cards that the port lacks now exist.

## Assumptions

- Decided with you in this session:
  - The hotbar hover keeps a tinted Glow image. The colors and durations come from the SWF and no longer from screenshots.
  - The green glow on an icon whose Window is open is removed. The original has no such state.
  - The hub's strings come from the extractor output, and the strings extractor is Part 7 of ui-foundation.
- The bottom bar body is shared with the Battle screen. This module builds it as its own scene, and the later battle HUD spec reuses it.
- Behavior stays as it is unless listed. In particular, the hotbar buttons still toggle their Window. The original's buttons only open a Window and do nothing while any Window or the card is open (`Krin.PauseForScreen`). That is behavior, and it is out of scope.
- Names and paths marked "proposed" are this spec's choice.

## Code style

As in ui-foundation. Scenes type their Stage numbers once, and the rect test checks them. This is the shape of the card's script:

```gdscript
class_name HubCard
extends Control

signal closed

func show_card(title: String, text: String) -> void:
	%Title.text = title
	%Body.text = text
	show()

func _on_close_button_pressed() -> void:
	hide()
	closed.emit()
```

## Testing strategy

- **Rect test.** Add `bottom_bar.tscn`, `hotbar.tscn`, `hub_card.tscn` and the seven zone scenes to the record. Every piece in the tables passes under 0.5 px, and every Label's size and color match.
- **Behavior (GUT).**
  - Each info spot opens the card with its own `NAVTITLE[n]` and `NAVTEXT[n]`, and the close button hides it.
  - The card blocks a click on a hotbar button.
  - Save shows the "Information" card.
  - `show_battle_hint` shows a hint for 100 to 104 and 504 once, and nothing for other battles.
  - The art hides while a Window shows and returns after.
  - An icon stays white while its Window is open.
  - The zone fill's width is `progress / max` of the track, capped, and the stage text is not capped.
- **Strings.** A test compares each hotbar tooltip with `strings.json`, so a typed string fails.
- **Extractors.** A second `uv run extract_hotbar_icons` leaves every PNG and the JSON byte-identical. For every icon, its rect times 5 equals its PNG size. `uv run pytest` covers the pure parts.
- **Must-fail controls.** Move one orb 20 px in a copy of a zone scene and expect the rect test to fail. Point one tooltip at the wrong `SYSTEM` index and expect the strings test to fail.
- **Compare tool.** An entry for frame 181 in `chrome` mode, scoring the bottom bar, the squares, the progress track and the art inside the masks. The ffdec export of frame 181 is solid black, because a full-stage black clip, 2837, covers it (papercut `ffdec frame exports of the hub and battle are solid black`). The entry renders through `render.py` with 2837 in the strip list. The bar comes from the first passing run, and the reference shifted 20 px scores over it.
- **Whole suite.** `uv run gut` passes before each commit.

## Boundaries

- Always: Take a SWF number, color or string from the rect record or an extractor. Keep `toggle_menu_screen` and the Window groups working. Commit `.png.import` sidecars.
- Ask first: A change to how the hotbar buttons behave while a Window is open. A change to the World map button's target. A new dependency.
- Never: Type a tooltip string, a glow color or a hover duration. Draw the bottom bar from `hotbar/background.png`. Add an Armor Games link (the strip 3176 with "www.armorgames.com" stays out under ADR 0002). Change the Battle screen's bar.

## Out of scope

- The World map Screen and its art.
- The notice banner (2852), the achievement banner (2836) and the fade-in (2837).
- The two off-stage debug buttons (3181, 3182).
- The tooltip's look.
- Inert-while-a-Window-is-open behavior of the hotbar buttons. Only the Victory Window's `set_inert` call is in scope.
- The Battle screen's bottom bar, the clock ring, the alert icon and the member bars.

## Success criteria

1. The bottom bar, the hotbar, the zone progress, the zone art and the orbs sit within 0.5 px of the SWF in all seven zones.
2. The hotbar draws from the new icon art, hover colors and durations come from the SWF, and no icon turns green when its Window opens.
3. Every hotbar and orb tooltip reads from `strings.json`.
4. Each of the 20 info spots opens the card with its own text. Save opens the card. The hint shows for battles 100 to 104 and 504 once.
5. The zone art hides while a Window shows and returns after.
6. `extract_hotbar_icons` is byte-identical on a second run, and the compare tool scores the hub entry under its bar with the 20 px control over it.
7. `uv run gut` passes.

## Open questions

1. **World Map's glow.** The icon uses a GradientGlow filter, which the inventory note did not decode. The extractor decodes it, and the hover needs one color. This spec suggests the outer gradient stop. The build issue shows the result against frame 7 and asks for your call.
2. **When `tutSpeecher` resets.** The flag limits the hint to once, and this spec did not read where it resets. The build issue reads it, and the port keeps that rule.
3. **The card's boxes.** The inventory note says the card's boxes are 2848's local boxes moved by its placement at (393.85, 286.00), and the dim box listed looks like a Stage box. The rect extractor gives the Stage rect, and the table above is a reference until the record confirms it.
4. **Hotbar toggle.** The buttons toggle their Window in the port, and the original's only open one. That is behavior and no spec covers it. Decide whether it gets its own issue.
